# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PPA Power Play Sport Club staff and owner, working in the back office (AdminConsole):

- **Front desk:** a counter computer during busy shifts. Confirms PromptPay slips, scans member QR codes, sells packages at the counter, answers members about bookings and rights.
- **Manager:** runs today's operations (court schedule, booking holds, maintenance), staff, content and prices.
- **Finance:** payments, reconciliation, reports.
- **Owner:** checks business health from a phone (revenue, members, packages sold).

Each role sees only what its permissions allow. Members use a separate LINE LIFF app, which is not this surface.

## Product Purpose

Run a multi-sport complex day to day. Nothing waiting for staff is missed (slips to confirm, holds about to lapse, memberships about to expire), today's courts and visits are visible at a glance, and the owner sees real revenue and membership numbers for stated periods (today, this month) instead of samples.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, professional. Numbers are exact and labelled with their period; actions say what they do. Thai first, English available.

## Anti-references

- "Command center" SaaS templates: hero metric banners, progress rings, gradient glows, charts added for decoration.
- Dashboards whose figures come from an arbitrary sample (for example the latest 80 rows) without a stated period.
- Walls of identical cards with an icon, a number and a label.

## Design Principles

1. **Action first.** What needs a person now sits at the top, with a count and the oldest wait.
2. **Every number has a period.** Today, this month, last 30 days; never an unlabeled total.
3. **Role-aware.** Show only what the signed-in staff member can act on; no empty panels for missing permissions.
4. **Calm density.** Plain figures and short lists over decoration; color is reserved for status.
5. **Counter and phone.** Scannable in seconds on a counter screen, one readable column on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast in both admin themes, status never conveyed by color alone (always a text label), every action reachable by keyboard, reduced motion respected. Thai and English labels, with line height suited to Thai script.
